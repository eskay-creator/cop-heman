About Children's Ministry

The Children’s Ministry aims to teach young minds about God’s word through Sunday School, storytelling, crafts, songs, and games that nurture faith and character.

Activities

  • Sunday School classes
  • Bible storytelling sessions
  • Creative arts and crafts
