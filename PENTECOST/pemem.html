About PEMEM

PEMEM seeks to empower men in the church spiritually and socially through prayer meetings, Bible studies, workshops, and community outreach programs. It emphasizes leadership and strengthening faith.

Activities

  • Weekly prayer meetings
  • Leadership training sessions
  • Community outreach programs
  • Bible study groups
